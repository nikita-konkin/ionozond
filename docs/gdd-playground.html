GDD on the ionogram

A model 1F trace for the Cyprus path, built on the dispersion measured over 583 sweeps. Set the MUF, then put a signal of some bandwidth anywhere on the trace. The page shows the trace's slope there, how far it spreads the band in delay, what that does to a pulse, and how much of it the console can resolve. The model. The lower 1F ray is τ(f) = 8.95 ms + b·MUF·(2(1 − √(1 − x)) − x), with x = f/MUF. That is the integral of the median law GDD = b·(1/√(1 − x) − 1), with b = 7.5 µs/MHz fitted to 583 Cyprus sweeps of February 2026. Real traces scatter about it by the interquartile range in Part 11's table, and a single sweep's measurement by about ±4 µs/MHz more. The upper ray and 2F are drawn for orientation and enter no number. The pulse is a Hann-shaped band of width B centred on the cursor, sent through the phase φ(f) = −2π∫(τ − τc) df that the trace implies. Fading, O/X splitting and absorption are left out. The console column is 41 kHz and its delay bin 24.4 µs, both at FFT 16 384 and 100 kHz/s.
